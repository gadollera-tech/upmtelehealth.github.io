The UP Manila National Telehealth Center convened the Philippine FHIR Connectathon in June 2026 to build, test, and validate the draft **Philippine Core** and **Philippine eReferral** implementation guides.

Participating teams tested a synthetic referral journey across primary, district, and apex facilities. The demonstrations showed how different electronic medical record systems could exchange referral information and status updates across organizations and locations.

The activity forms part of the **Strengthening Standards Capability Project**, a multi-year collaboration supporting standards-based digital health interoperability. The implementation guides remain draft artifacts under active development and are not yet intended for production use.
