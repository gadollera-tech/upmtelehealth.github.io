The Community Health Information Tracking System, developed by the UP Manila National Telehealth Center, is one of the country's longest-running electronic medical record systems for government primary-care facilities.

Technology transfer and licensing arrangements were developed to support wider deployment, implementation, training, and technical assistance for rural health units and other eligible facilities.

CHITS illustrates how publicly developed research and software can move from a university project toward sustainable use in health services.
