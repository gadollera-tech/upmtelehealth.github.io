The FHIR Structured Data Capture training introduced developers and health IT specialists to standards-based forms that can guide inputs, perform calculations, apply conditional logic, and reuse existing patient information.

The training was offered under the **Strengthening Standards Capability Project** as the Philippines continues work toward more interoperable health information exchange.

Participants were expected to have prior familiarity with FHIR, JSON, REST APIs, and FHIRPath expressions.
