The UP Manila National Telehealth Center marked its 25th founding anniversary in September 2023, bringing together leaders and partners to reflect on its contributions and future direction.

The celebration highlighted landmark initiatives including **CHITS**, the **RxBox**, the National Telehealth Service Program, and **UP Manila BESTS**.

The milestone reaffirmed the center's commitment to practical and equitable digital health innovation for the Philippines.
