The National Telehealth Center led a multi-sector Road Safety Connectathon in November 2025 to develop interoperable systems for road emergency and crash data management.

Participants harmonized more than 200 data elements and worked toward standardized minimum data sets and interoperability guidelines using the HL7 FHIR standard.

The intended outcome is more seamless information flow among first responders, hospitals, and national surveillance systems, supporting both patient care and evidence-informed road-safety policy.
