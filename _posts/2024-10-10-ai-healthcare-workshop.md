The National Telehealth Center, the UP College of Medicine Medical Informatics Unit, and Senti AI conducted a hands-on workshop on artificial intelligence in healthcare during the 26th NIH Anniversary Conference.

The activity covered text analysis, medical imaging, generative AI, vision AI, and the ethical implications of using AI in health settings.

The workshop demonstrated NTHC's role in building digital and AI literacy among health professionals, faculty members, and IT practitioners.
