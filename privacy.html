Privacy Notice

This page is a working privacy-notice template for the proposed UP Manila National Telehealth Center website. It must be reviewed and approved by the appropriate UP Manila data protection and legal offices before publication.

Information collected

The website’s inquiry and updates forms may collect a visitor’s name, email address, organization, role, sector, areas of interest, inquiry type, message, and consent preferences.

Purpose

Submitted information may be used to respond to inquiries, assess collaboration requests, administer events or training, maintain an opt-in contact list, and send public NTHC news and opportunities.

Do not submit health information

The forms are not intended for clinical consultation or patient referral. Visitors should not submit patient names, medical records, diagnoses, images, or other sensitive personal and health information.

Storage, access, and retention

Before deployment, NTHC should document where form submissions are stored, who may access them, how long they are retained, the approved deletion schedule, and the security controls applied by the selected hosting and form-processing services.

Consent and withdrawal

Joining the updates list is voluntary. Subscribers should be given a clear way to withdraw consent or unsubscribe from future communications.

Rights and contact

Requests concerning access, correction, deletion, objection, or other data-subject rights should be routed to the approved UP Manila privacy contact. Replace this paragraph with the institution’s official data protection contact details before launch.

Recommended pre-launch review

  • Confirm the lawful basis and approved purpose for every form field.
  • Use only an institutionally approved form processor and hosting provider.
  • Configure role-based access, multifactor authentication, and retention rules.
  • Publish the final privacy notice and incident-reporting contact.
  • Conduct a privacy and security review before collecting real visitor data.
