Privacy

Draft website privacy notice.

This text is a working draft and must be reviewed by the appropriate UP Manila and NTHC privacy and legal officers before public launch.

Information collected

The website may collect contact details voluntarily submitted through newsletter or inquiry forms, including name, email address, organization, interests, and message content.

Purpose

Submitted information may be used to respond to inquiries, communicate NTHC news and opportunities, manage event interest, and evaluate website engagement.

Important limitation

Do not submit patient records, clinical images, diagnoses, medical histories, or other personal health information through the public website forms.

Storage and access

If the included Google Apps Script workflow is enabled, submissions may be stored in an institution-controlled Google Sheet. Access, retention, deletion, security, and breach-response rules must be finalized before deployment.

Your choices

Visitors should be able to request correction, deletion, or withdrawal from contact-list messages using the official NTHC email address.

Contact

Questions about this draft notice may be sent to nih-nthc.upmanila@up.edu.ph.
