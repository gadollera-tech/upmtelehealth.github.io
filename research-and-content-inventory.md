## 3. Program areas

The legacy NTHC website presents five major program areas, while the current NIH profile and recent projects show a strong expansion into standards and interoperability.

1. **eLearning and capacity building**
   - Seminars, workshops, training, and eHealth education for health workers, educators, developers, researchers, and the public.
2. **eMedicine and telemedicine**
   - Remote clinical referral and specialist support for geographically isolated and underserved communities.
3. **eRecords and health information systems**
   - Electronic medical records, registries, reporting, and information systems, including CHITS.
4. **eHealth policy advocacy and networks**
   - Policy development, stakeholder convening, regional collaboration, and advocacy for responsible digital health.
5. **eSurveillance and data use**
   - Mobile and web systems for monitoring public-health programs, local governance, and real-time reporting.
6. **Standards and interoperability**
   - Current work on FHIR, implementation guides, terminology, eReferral, national health information exchange, and standards capability.

## 4. Current and recent initiatives

### Strengthening Standards Capability Project (SSCP)
A multi-year initiative housed at UP NTHC, co-funded by CSIRO Australia and the Australian Government Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade through Australian Aid. It develops and tests national health data standards, hosts connectathons, and supports Philippine Core and eReferral FHIR Implementation Guides.

Source:
- https://www.upm.edu.ph/cpt_news/philippine-emr-systems-achieve-live-health-data-exchange-at-june-2026-fhir-connectathon-in-aklan/

### Philippine Core and Philippine eReferral FHIR Implementation Guides
Draft implementation guides being built and tested to enable consistent, secure exchange of patient and referral data. The June 2026 Aklan Connectathon demonstrated live exchange among Philippine EMR systems.

Source:
- https://www.upm.edu.ph/cpt_news/philippine-emr-systems-achieve-live-health-data-exchange-at-june-2026-fhir-connectathon-in-aklan/

### National Release Center Webinar Series
Launched in June/July 2026 as a knowledge-sharing series concerning health-data quality, standards, interoperability, and implementation resources. Confirm the final official program description before production publication.

Source:
- https://www.facebook.com/UPMTelehealth/

### Road Safety Data Interoperability
NTHC convened agencies, health experts, emergency responders, academe, and industry to harmonize road-safety data. The November 2025 Connectathon worked on more than 200 data elements, standardized minimum data sets, and FHIR-based interoperability guidelines.

Sources:
- https://www.upm.edu.ph/cpt_news/connectathon-national-telehealth-center-concerned-sectors-convene-to-create-road-safety-data-systems/
- https://www.upm.edu.ph/cpt_news/nthc-moves-to-harmonize-road-crash-data-for-improved-reporting/

### FHIR Structured Data Capture Training
A May 2026 technical training for software developers, programmers, and health IT specialists under SSCP, focusing on standardized smart forms, logic, auto-population, and FHIRPath.

Source:
- https://www.upm.edu.ph/cpt_announcements/building-the-brain-phie/

### AI in Healthcare workshops
NTHC, the UP College of Medicine Medical Informatics Unit, and Senti AI conducted an introductory hands-on workshop in September 2024 covering generative AI, vision AI, medical applications, and ethics.

Source:
- https://www.upm.edu.ph/cpt_news/up-manila-hosts-inaugural-workshop-on-artificial-intelligence-in-healthcare/

### Familial Hypercholesterolemia Registry
An NTHC-supported registry portal for the Philippine Lipid and Atherosclerosis Society.

Source:
- https://plas.telehealth.ph/

## 5. Landmark and foundational projects

### CHITS
The Community Health Information Tracking System is a pioneering open-source electronic medical record developed for government health facilities, community health centers, and rural health units. It supports patient records, reporting, and claims-related workflows and later contributed to the creation of a UP Manila digital-health spin-off.

Sources:
- https://telehealth.ph/program-areas/
- https://www.upm.edu.ph/cpt_news/up-manilas-long-running-e-med-record-system-licensee-to-capacitate-more-rhus-other-health-facilities/

### RxBox
A biomedical telemedicine device capable of measuring vital signs and transmitting data via internet and GSM, supporting geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas and specialist referral.

Source:
- https://www.upm.edu.ph/cpt_news/pioneering-telemedicine-and-digital-health-solutions-in-the-philippines/

### rCHITS
Real-time monitoring of maternal and child health indicators in geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas, with extensions to governance, civil registration, social protection, and disaster-related reporting.

Sources:
- https://telehealth.ph/program-areas/
- https://telehealth.ph/ongoing-research/

### National Telehealth Service Program
Telemedicine research and service connecting remote health providers to specialists through practical communication technologies.

Sources:
- https://telehealth.ph/research/
- https://telehealth.ph/ongoing-research/

### UP Manila BESTS
An open-source web application developed during the COVID-19 pandemic to track employee health status and identify potential hotspots, supporting institutional guidelines and policy.

Source:
- https://www.upm.edu.ph/cpt_news/pioneering-telemedicine-and-digital-health-solutions-in-the-philippines/

### Asia eHealth Information Network
NTHC historically served as a convening and secretariat partner in regional digital-health collaboration and capacity building.

Sources:
- https://telehealth.ph/program-areas/
- https://aehin.org/

## 7. Branding foundation

The University of the Philippines Visual Identity Guidebook identifies:

- **UP Maroon** as the primary institutional color
- **Forest Green** as the complementary institutional color
- **Gold/yellow** as an accent color
- **Spot black** for the logotype
- Optima for titles/headings and Palatino for official body text; Avenir/Helvetica may be used for unofficial materials
- The seal/logotype should be placed at the upper left of a website and must not be redrawn, distorted, recolored, or modified

Website implementation uses:

- UP Maroon: `#7B1113`
- Forest Green: `#014421`
- Gold accent: `#F4C430`
- Restrained blue only for NTHC/digital-health graphics
- Optima-style system fallbacks for headings and Avenir/Helvetica-style system fallbacks for body text

## 8. Lead-capture strategy

The site has two explicit, consent-based collection points:

### Updates/contact-list form
- First name
- Email
- Interest category
- Opt-in consent

### Inquiry form
- Full name
- Email
- Organization
- Role
- Sector
- Inquiry type
- Message
- Processing consent
- Optional updates opt-in

Recommended inquiry categories:
- Research collaboration
- Standards and interoperability
- Training or speaking request
- Telemedicine / digital health systems
- Partnership or funding
- Student research / internship
- Media inquiry
- General inquiry

The forms explicitly prohibit submission of patient information or personal health data.
