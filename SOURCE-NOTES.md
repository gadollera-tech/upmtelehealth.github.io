# Source and verification notes

Public content in this prototype was compiled from the official UP Manila website, the NIH NTHC profile, Telehealth.ph, PubMed, and PubMed Central.

Before launch, NTHC should verify:

- Complete staff roster, titles, bios, emails, and any profile photos.
- Which projects are active, completed, paused, or under proposal.
- Complete institutional publication list.
- Current career openings and application instructions.
- Future events and registration links.
- Privacy notice, data retention, Google Sheet ownership, access controls, and consent language.
- Official social media links and communications approval workflow.

Key public source pages used include the NIH NTHC profile, UP Manila reports on the 2026 Philippine FHIR Connectathon, FHIR SDC training, the 2025 Road Safety Connectathon, CHITS technology transfer, AI in Healthcare workshop, and NTHC's 25th anniversary.
