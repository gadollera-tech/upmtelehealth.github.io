About NTHC

Digital health, public service, and research that serve people.

The UP Manila National Telehealth Center advances digital health through research, health information systems, interoperability, standards, implementation support, and public service. It works with academic, government, healthcare, and community partners to build practical digital solutions that strengthen healthcare delivery and evidence-informed decision-making.

Who we are

A university-based center focused on digital health innovation.

NTHC is a center under the UP Manila National Institutes of Health. Its work spans digital health applications, health information systems, interoperability, standards, registries, capacity-building, and research translation.

Through collaborative and multidisciplinary work, the center supports initiatives that improve access to health information, strengthen health systems, and respond to the evolving digital-health needs of institutions, practitioners, and communities.

Research and innovation

Project-driven, collaborative, and multidisciplinary.

NTHC's work is carried by teams across research, implementation, systems development, policy support, interoperability, public health, and institutional collaboration. The center's project portfolio includes standards work, registries, surveillance, maternal and child health, artificial intelligence, and health information systems.
