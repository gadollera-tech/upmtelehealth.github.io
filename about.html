About the center

Digital health research shaped by people, systems, and public purpose.

Created on 30 July 1998, NTHC advances transformative digital health research and development through interdisciplinary, translational, and community-engaged work.

Mandate

Design, develop, implement, and evaluate digital health solutions.

The center prioritizes electronic medical and health records, public-health surveillance, telemedicine, standards, interoperability, and workforce capacity.

Its work connects research, implementation, monitoring and evaluation, policy, training, and public service to improve health care delivery.

Vision

eHealth in empowered communities.

Mission

Engage communities in appropriate and progressive ICTs toward better equity in health.

Approach

Innovative, interdisciplinary, translational, ethical, and partnership-driven.
