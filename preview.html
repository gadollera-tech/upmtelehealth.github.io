Who we are

eHealth in empowered communities.

Created on 30 July 1998, NTHC serves as the digital health research and development center of the UP Manila National Institutes of Health.

Its mission is to engage communities in the design, development, and use of appropriate and progressive information and communication technologies toward better equity in health.

Program areas

Building the foundations of connected care.

From frontline service delivery to national policy and standards, NTHC works across the digital health ecosystem.

01

Standards & interoperability

Developing and testing health data standards, implementation guides, terminology, and exchange workflows that help information systems communicate securely.

FHIR · HIE · eReferral
02

eMedicine & telemedicine

Connecting patients and frontline providers in underserved communities with clinical expertise through appropriate telehealth technologies.

Remote care · Referral
03

eRecords & health systems

Designing and evaluating electronic medical records, registries, and health information systems that support continuity, reporting, and better decisions.

EMR · Registries
04

eLearning & capacity building

Equipping health professionals, government teams, developers, educators, and researchers through seminars, workshops, and hands-on training.

Training · Workshops
05

Policy & digital health networks

Convening stakeholders and contributing evidence, policy guidance, and regional networks that strengthen responsible digital health governance.

Policy · Partnerships
06

Surveillance & data use

Applying mobile and web-based systems to monitor public health programs, support local governance, and turn data into timely action.

mHealth · Dashboards

FoundationaleRecords

CHITS

The Community Health Information Tracking System is a pioneering open-source electronic medical record developed for community health centers and rural health units.

Learn about CHITS ↗
FoundationalTelemedicine device

RxBox

A biomedical telemedicine device that measures vital signs and transmits clinical data, supporting remote communities and specialist referral.

FoundationalMaternal & child health

rCHITS

Real-time monitoring of maternal and child health indicators, extending digital tools to governance, civil registration, and community programs.
